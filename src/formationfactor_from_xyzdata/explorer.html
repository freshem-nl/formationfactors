The fitted lines are expressed directly as EC bulk,25 = A + B × chloride[g/L], using EC groundwater,25 = 1.25 + (1000/360) × chloride. Thus A = a + 1.25b and B = (1000/360)b. The purple constraint remains fixed at minimum bulk EC at zero groundwater EC, not at zero chloride.

Formation factor & surface conductivity at 25 °C

Model: EC bulk,25 = EC groundwater,25 / F + σsurface,25. For the fitted relation y = a + b × x: F = 1/b (dimensionless), σsurface,25 = a (mS/cm). Divide by 10 for S/m. Purple fit: σsurface,25 is fixed to the minimum bulk EC of the relevant subset.

Formation-factor intervals transform each bootstrap slope as 1/b and are suppressed when the slope interval reaches or falls below zero. Fixed-minimum surface-conductivity intervals collapse to the chosen constant by construction; they do not imply that physical surface conductivity is known exactly. Negative surface conductivity and F < 1 are reported, not clipped or constrained. Pooled formations and sources need not share one formation factor.

OLS fits EC bulk,25 = intercept + slope × EC groundwater,25 in original units. Axes are swapped relative to the earlier app; both regressions are fitted in this new forward direction, not algebraically inverted. The fixed-minimum fit uses a = min(EC bulk,25) of its own before/after subset and b = Σ[x(y − a)] / Σx². The minimum is recalculated after exclusions. R² = 1 − SSE/SST uses the same centred SST for both models and can be negative for a fixed intercept. 95% pointwise mean bands use 1,500 source-stratified whole-log bootstrap draws (fixed seed). The fixed-minimum bootstrap holds the observed minimum constant: its uncertainty is conditional on that chosen minimum, not uncertainty in the minimum itself. At least 3 logs are required for bootstrap intervals. Lines extend to x = 0 on linear axes to show intercepts; this is extrapolation outside the observed groundwater-EC range. RMSE is in-sample, not a prediction interval. Conversion-factor uncertainty is not included.
Exclusion is a sensitivity experiment, not evidence that a measurement is wrong. Confidence intervals after data-driven exclusions are exploratory and do not account for the selection process, lithology, measurement bias, time differences or spatial matching uncertainty.
